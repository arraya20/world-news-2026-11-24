An original, five-story editorial poster about news reported on Tuesday, 24 November 2026. The SVG uses only hand-drawn geometry and live text. No photographs, logos, maps, or files from another entry were copied. The headline hierarchy was designed to remain readable when the 1024 × 1024 image is displayed at about 300 pixels wide.

## The five stories

1. Iran's Revolutionary Guard Navy said three tankers caught fire after hitting naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz. The poster attributes the claim to Iran.[1]
2. Officials said a Russian strike on Zaporizhzhia, Ukraine, killed two civilians and wounded 48, including four children.[2]
3. China unveiled its Shenzhou 24 crew on 24 November; Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali is scheduled to fly with two Chinese astronauts.[3]
4. New UN figures published on 24 November estimated that 47,000 women and girls were killed by intimate partners or family members in 2025.[4]
5. On 24 November, WHO announced US$2 million to support Cuba's immunization activities and said it had provided medical equipment to health facilities.[5]

The poster says *crew revealed* rather than depicting the Shenzhou launch, which was scheduled for 25 November. Its UN number is clearly labeled as a 2025 figure released on 24 November.

## Run it

Requires Python 3.13 and only its standard library. The submitted program copies the checked image without network access.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`artwork.svg` is the editable source. `entry.png` is its rendered output. The program writes `/output/entry.png` in the make.fun sandbox.
